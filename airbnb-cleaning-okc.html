Why Short-Term Rental Cleaning Is Different

Guest turnover has a hard deadline: the next guest arrives in hours, and a single missed detail can cost a 5-star rating. Short-term rental (STR) cleaning is faster, more consistent and more detail-driven than a regular home clean — and most OKC hosts can't run it themselves while managing bookings, maintenance and guests.

The Turnaround Checklist

  • ✔ All laundry: bedding, towels, bath mats — replaced and made beds
  • ✔ Kitchen reset: dishes, counters, sink, appliance exteriors
  • ✔ Bathrooms sanitized: toilet, shower, sink, mirrors, floors
  • ✔ Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • ✔ Dusting: furniture, shelves, window sills
  • ✔ Restock supplies: toilet paper, soap, paper towels, coffee
  • ✔ Final walkthrough with photos — ready for the next guest

Deep Cleaning Between Bookings

Even with perfect turnarounds, an STR needs a monthly or quarterly deep clean: inside the oven and fridge, baseboards, ceiling fans, under beds, grout. This keeps the property from degrading and prevents issues guests complain about. See what a professional deep clean covers.
