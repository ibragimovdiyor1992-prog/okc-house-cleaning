10 House Cleaning Tips from OKC Professionals

Real tips our Oklahoma City cleaners use every day — so your home stays cleaner between visits.

Get Your Free Estimate

1. Clean Top to Bottom, Left to Right

Dust falls. Start with ceiling fans and shelves, finish with floors — and you never clean the same surface twice. It's the single biggest time-saver professional cleaners use.

2. Give Cleaning Products 5-10 Minutes to Work

Spray the bathroom and kitchen first, then move to another room. By the time you're back, the product has dissolved soap scum and grease — wiping takes half the effort.

3. Microfiber Cloths Beat Paper Towels

Microfiber traps dust instead of pushing it around, works with plain water, and washes hundreds of times. Keep one color for kitchens and another for bathrooms to avoid cross-contamination.

4. Make the Bed First

It instantly makes the room look 50% cleaner and takes 60 seconds. It's also a psychological win — the room looks done, so you keep going.

5. Vacuum in One Direction, Then the Other

Two passes at 90 degrees lifts more embedded dirt from carpets — especially in high-traffic entryways. If you vacuum weekly, alternate directions each week.

6. Degrease the Kitchen Hood and Stove Top Weekly

Grease buildup is the #1 thing OKC homeowners miss. A 10-minute degrease each week prevents the sticky film that turns into a deep-cleaning project.

7. Descale Showerheads and Faucets Monthly

Oklahoma City has hard water. Soak showerheads and faucets in vinegar monthly — it keeps them flowing and sparkling, and prevents mineral stains in the shower.

8. Use a Squeegee After Every Shower

Ten seconds with a squeegee prevents soap scum and hard-water spots — the difference between a weekly wipe-down and a monthly scrubbing session.

9. Declutter Before You Clean

Cleaning around clutter takes twice as long. Keep a basket for each room: toss in what doesn't belong, put it away at the end. Cleaning gets faster every week.
