Why Fall Cleaning Matters in Oklahoma City

Oklahoma summers are dusty and allergy-heavy. A fall reset clears the season's residue — pollen, dust and grit — before windows close for winter. It's also the perfect time for a deep cleaning, so you enter the holiday season with a clean base.

Inside: The Fall Reset

  • ✔ Vacuum and steam-clean carpets — summer tracked in more dirt than you think
  • ✔ Wash windows inside and out while it's still warm enough
  • ✔ Clean window screens and tracks
  • ✔ Dust ceiling fans one last time before they rest for winter
  • ✔ Deep-clean the oven and fridge before holiday cooking begins
  • ✔ Flip and vacuum mattresses
  • ✔ Wash comforters and seasonal bedding

Outside: What OKC Homes Need Before Winter

  • ✔ Clear gutters of leaves and debris — Oklahoma storms test them early
  • ✔ Sweep porches, patios and garage floors
  • ✔ Wipe down outdoor furniture before storing it
  • ✔ Check weatherstripping on doors and windows

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I schedule fall cleaning?

September to mid-October is ideal — before holiday cooking and guests, while windows can still be opened for airing out.

Is fall cleaning the same as deep cleaning?

A fall clean is a deep cleaning plus seasonal tasks (windows, screens, carpets, gutters). Most OKC families book the deep clean and add carpet and window services.

Do I need to be home for the cleaning?

No — we work with keys and garage codes. You come home to a fall-ready house.
