Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book a deep cleaning just once?

Absolutely. One-time deep cleaning is our most popular service for new clients. There's no obligation to continue.

What's the difference between deep and standard cleaning?

Deep cleaning covers more areas: inside appliances, baseboards, window tracks, light fixtures, and detailed bathroom scrubbing. Standard cleaning covers surfaces, floors, and daily maintenance.

How long does a one-time deep clean take?

A 2-bedroom home typically takes 3-4 hours for a deep clean. Add 1-2 hours per additional bedroom.
