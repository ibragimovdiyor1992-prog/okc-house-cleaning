Maid Service vs House Cleaning in OKC

The words get used interchangeably, but there's a real difference. A maid or housekeeper is hired for regular, ongoing help — light cleaning, tidying, sometimes laundry and organization. A house cleaning service sends a team for a thorough, room-by-room clean of your home. In Oklahoma City, most families book a cleaning team for standard, deep or move-out cleaning — and it costs less per visit than a dedicated maid.
