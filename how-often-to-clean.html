Why a Cleaning Schedule Matters

When cleaning has no schedule, it only happens when the house is already overwhelming. A simple routine spreads the work out — 10 minutes here, 30 minutes there — so no single day is a marathon. Most Oklahoma City families find that a mix of daily habits, a weekly reset and a monthly deeper pass keeps a home genuinely clean.

Daily Tasks (5-10 Minutes)

  • ✔ Make the bed
  • ✔ Wash dishes or load the dishwasher
  • ✔ Wipe kitchen counters
  • ✔ Quick sweep or vacuum of high-traffic areas
  • ✔ Clear clutter from tables and counters

Ten minutes a day prevents the 'Sunday pile-up' that makes cleaning feel impossible.

Weekly Tasks (1-2 Hours)

  • ✔ Bathrooms: toilet, shower, sink, mirror
  • ✔ Kitchen: stove top, sink, counters, floors
  • ✔ Vacuum and mop all floors
  • ✔ Dust surfaces and baseboards
  • ✔ Change bedding
  • ✔ Take out trash and recycling

If weekly cleaning feels like too much on top of work and family, this is exactly when OKC families book a professional cleaning team — weekly or bi-weekly visits run $120-330 and remove the whole chore from your week.

Monthly Tasks

  • ✔ Clean inside the oven and microwave
  • ✔ Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
  • ✔ Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • ✔ Wash windowsills and window tracks
  • ✔ Vacuum under furniture and sofa cushions
  • ✔ Organize closets and pantry

Seasonal and Yearly Deep Cleaning

Twice a year (spring and fall) most homes need a deep cleaning — baseboards, inside appliances, grout, everything a normal week skips. A professional deep clean in Oklahoma City runs $250-450. See exactly what's included in our deep cleaning checklist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need weekly professional cleaning?

No — weekly is for families who want it handled completely. Bi-weekly or monthly visits still keep the home noticeably cleaner than going it alone.

How long does a professional clean take?

1-2 hours for a 2-bedroom standard clean, 3-5 hours for a deep or move-out clean with a team of two.

Is it cheaper to clean my own house?

In dollars, yes. In time, no — a full weekly clean takes most people 2-4 hours. Many OKC families see professional cleaning as buying their weekend back.
