Frequently Asked Questions

Is end-of-lease cleaning the same as move-out cleaning?

Yes. We use both terms interchangeably. It's the comprehensive cleaning required when leaving a rental property.

Do you clean the property after I've moved furniture out?

Yes. End-of-lease cleaning is best done after all furniture is removed for full access to walls, floors, and baseboards.

Can you provide a receipt for my landlord?

Yes. We provide a detailed cleaning receipt you can submit to your landlord as proof of professional cleaning.
