Why a Move-In Clean Makes Sense

No matter how clean the previous tenants left it, a home gets a professional reset before your furniture and boxes go in. A move-in cleaning is a deep clean done on an empty property — so every corner, cabinet and closet is reachable. You start with a genuinely clean home instead of someone else's idea of clean.

What's Included

  • ✔ Kitchen: inside and outside cabinets, appliances, countertops, sink
  • ✔ Bathrooms: shower, tub, toilet, vanity, mirrors — sanitized
  • ✔ Floors: vacuumed, then mopped or hard-floor cleaned
  • ✔ Windowsills, window tracks and doors
  • ✔ Closets: vacuumed, wiped, ready for your things
  • ✔ Baseboards and light fixtures dusted

Move-In vs Move-Out Cleaning

The two are close cousins with different goals. A move-out cleaning targets what landlords check — so you get your deposit back. A move-in cleaning targets what you live with every day: inside cabinets, closet floors, behind doors. If you're renting in OKC, many landlords require a professional move-out clean anyway — and booking a move-in clean for the new place on the same day is common.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I clean before or after moving in?

Before. Cleaning an empty home is faster, cheaper and covers everything — cabinets, closets, behind appliances. After moving in, those spots are blocked by furniture.

Is move-in cleaning worth it?

For most people, yes: it removes dust, allergens and the previous occupants' residue before your family lives with it. It also means you unpack into a clean home, not a project.

Can you clean before my furniture arrives?

That's exactly how we recommend it. Let us know your move-in date and we'll schedule the clean a day or two ahead.
