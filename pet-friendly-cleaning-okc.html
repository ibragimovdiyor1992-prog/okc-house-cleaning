Cleaning a Home With Pets Is Different

Pet hair, dander, tracked-in dirt and the occasional accident — homes with dogs and cats need more vacuuming, more frequent surface cleaning, and products that are safe for animals. Our Oklahoma City team adapts the routine to your pets, never the other way around.

What We Do Differently in Pet Homes

  • ✔ Extra vacuum passes on furniture and under beds — the fur hotspots
  • ✔ Lint-roller treatment on upholstery and curtains
  • ✔ Pet-safe, eco-friendly products on request — no harsh residue
  • ✔ Odor-neutralizing treatment on carpets and fabrics
  • ✔ Food and water bowl areas cleaned and sanitized
  • ✔ We work around your pets — crates, rooms or schedules as you prefer

Keeping Allergies Under Control

For allergy-prone households we focus on the dander traps: bedding, upholstery, rugs and the spots pets sleep. Weekly or bi-weekly visits keep dander from building up — a huge difference for allergy sufferers. Add a deep cleaning every 6-12 months to reset the home.

What OKC Pet Owners Pay

Pet homes usually add $15-30 per visit on top of standard rates: standard from $120, deep from $250, move-out from $300. We always agree on the exact price before starting — no surprises. Full pricing by home size: Oklahoma City price guide.

We're pet people — insured, background-checked cleaners who are used to enthusiastic greeters at the door. Tell us about your pets when you book, and we'll make sure the visit goes smoothly for everyone (including the cat hiding under the bed).
