Deep Cleaning vs Standard Cleaning

A standard cleaning keeps an already-clean home tidy. A deep cleaning resets the home: every corner, every surface, everything you skip during a normal week. Most OKC homeowners do one deep clean every 6-12 months — and always before moving in or out.

Kitchen

  • ✔ Inside and outside of appliances (oven, fridge, microwave)
  • ✔ Cabinet fronts, handles and kickboards
  • ✔ Countertops scrubbed and sanitized
  • ✔ Sink descaled and polished
  • ✔ Backsplash degreased

Bathrooms

  • ✔ Shower, tub and tiles scrubbed and descaled
  • ✔ Toilets sanitized inside and out
  • ✔ Vanity, mirror and fixtures polished
  • ✔ Grout spot-cleaned
  • ✔ Exhaust fan dusted

Living Areas and Bedrooms

  • ✔ Baseboards and door frames wiped
  • ✔ Light switches and outlets cleaned
  • ✔ Ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted
  • ✔ Windowsills and window tracks
  • ✔ Furniture dusted top to bottom
  • ✔ Floors vacuumed, then mopped or hard-floored cleaned

How Much Does a Deep Clean Cost in OKC?

Expect $250-450 depending on home size. A 2-bedroom home averages $280-380. Add $50-100 if the home has heavy buildup or hasn't been cleaned in months. Prices are estimates — the final price depends on the scope of work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deep cleaning take?

A 2-bedroom home takes 3-5 hours with a team of two. Larger homes or heavy buildup can take longer.

How often should I deep clean?

Every 6-12 months, plus before moving in or out, after renovations, or when allergies act up.

Should I be home?

No — we work with keys and garage codes. You'll come home to a reset house.
